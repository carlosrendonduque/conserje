What happens to a lead

The widget holds no key, no prompt and no score. The backend talks to Claude, scores what it hears, and hands the lead to n8n, which notifies you, answers the visitor and logs the row. Step through any of the three paths below.

    Why the score is code

    The model extracts facts; the server turns them into a number. A model asked for a score drifts between runs and can be talked into a higher one. Weights in scorer.ts give the same tier for the same answers, and a change to them shows up in a diff.

    Why n8n answers first

    Anything but a prompt 2xx is a failed delivery, and failed deliveries are replayed. A workflow that alerted you and then failed would be re-sent every hour. So it answers the moment the lead is authenticated, and the replay gives up after a day.
